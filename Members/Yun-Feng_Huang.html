Research and Background

Profile

Yun-Feng Huang is a Researcher in the Department of Optics and Optical Engineering at the University of Science and Technology of China. His research interests include quantum communication, photonic quantum information processing, and trapped-ion quantum information processing.

Education

  • 1998-2003, Ph.D. program, Department of Physics, University of Science and Technology of China
  • 1994-1998, B.S., Department of Physics, University of Science and Technology of China

Experience

  • 2009-present, Researcher, Department of Optics and Optical Engineering, University of Science and Technology of China
  • 2006-2008, Associate Professor, Department of Physics, University of Science and Technology of China
  • 2003-2005, Lecturer, Department of Physics, University of Science and Technology of China

Research

Current work focuses on building quantum networks based on trapped-ion systems, including enhanced trapped-ion nodes, photonic quantum channels and hybrid quantum networks. His group has carried out experimental studies with Yb and Ba ions confined in linear ion traps.
