Super-resolved Imaging of a Single Cold Atom on a Nanosecond Timescale

The study combines ground-state depletion (GSD) microscopy with quantum-state transition control to achieve 2D super-resolved imaging of a single trapped ion, realizing a spatial resolution of 175 nm (a 13-fold improvement) and a temporal resolution of 50 ns.

Paper link: Physical Review Letters 127, 263603 (2021)
