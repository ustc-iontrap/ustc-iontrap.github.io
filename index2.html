Research

Trapped-ion quantum computing node

From low-crosstalk dual-sided addressing to single-sided Magnus gates, with wavefront correction, axial modes and optical-tweezer phonon control.

Read more

Quantum network with cavity QED

Developing high-bandwidth, high-efficiency ion–photon interfaces through fiber microcavity fabrication, mode matching, metal shielding, and stable cavity control.

Read more

Hybrid quantum network

Building on long ion coherence and high-fidelity control to connect solid-state memories, develop neutral-atom interfaces and enable distributed quantum computing across physical systems.
